## Key Logic Summary

The `MandateEvidencePacket` assembles a **policy / industry / company three-layer** evidence view per left-side candidate:
- **Policy layer**: resolved topic, policy level (central/ministry/local), lifecycle status, deduplicated evidence titles from `policy_evidence_refs` + `mandate_evidence_refs` + topic registry `evidence_links`
- **Industry layer**: matched chain segments (beneficiary_path ∩ defined industry chain), beneficiary path
- **Company layer**: normalized `CompanyRole` enum + preserved raw descriptive label (e.g. "动力系统"), raw_evidence availability detection (announcements/research_report/news — status+record_count only, no free-text parsing)
- `needs_manual_research=True` when policy OR company layer is thin — prevents inflating weak candidates
- `confidence` (high/medium/low) is descriptive only, never a buy signal
- No LLM, no fabricated conclusions, no forbidden trade words
