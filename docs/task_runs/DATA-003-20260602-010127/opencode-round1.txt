### Key Logic

- **Multi-source normalization**: Accepts `EventItem`, raw `dict`, or any attribute-bearing object → unified `MandateSignal` via `_raw_event_to_mandate_signal()`
- **Research report isolation**: 30+ broker/research keywords force `source_level=MEDIA` — research reports can never become CENTRAL/STATE_COUNCIL even if their title mentions policy
- **Company announcement isolation**: `event_type=notice` without policy keywords → `COMPANY_NOTICE`, cannot auto-promote to policy level
- **Title deduplication**: Same title across multiple sources deduplicated by `(symbol, normalized_title)`, keeping the highest-authority source and merging all `raw_refs` and `policy_tags`
- **Topic tagging**: Reuses H-002 `match_topics()` vocabulary for automatic `policy_tags`
- **Direction inference**: Priority: `event_type_hint` (buyback→bullish, rating→keyword-based) → title keyword patterns → default neutral
- **Convenience APIs**: `normalize_event_source_result()`, `normalize_event_items_by_symbol()`, `normalize_raw_dicts()` for direct consumption from different upstream sources
