### Key Logic

**6 Candidate Types**: `POLICY_AMBUSH` (left-side ambush, no breakout needed), `POLICY_CONFIRM` (right-side with tech confirmation), `TECH_TRADE` (pure technical, short-term), `EVENT_WATCH` (weak signals, observe), `PSEUDO_POLICY` (concept-only noise), `OVERHEATED_AVOID` (risk-crowded, avoid).

**Ambush Score** = mandate 35% + beneficiary 30% + pricing_gap 20% - overheat_penalty 15%. POLICY_AMBUSH requires mandate_score_component >= 40 AND beneficiary_score_component >= 35 AND no breakout.

**Deep TA Routing**: `POLICY_AMBUSH` → policy_verify queue; `POLICY_CONFIRM` → deep_ta; `TECH_TRADE` → short_term; others → skip/observe.
